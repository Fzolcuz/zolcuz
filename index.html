How We Work

From Brief to Live in 21 Days

  1. 01
    Days 1–3

    Discovery & Strategy

    We immerse in your brand, study your competitors, and map the exact story your site must tell. You leave this phase with a signed creative brief, a content outline, and a clear visual direction before a single pixel is designed.

  2. 02
    Days 4–7

    Design & Animation

    High-fidelity screens, motion concepts, and the full interaction language are built in parallel. Every scroll effect, entrance, and transition is engineered to serve the story, not decorate it. One round of consolidated feedback, then we lock.

  3. 03
    Days 8–17

    Build & Integration

    Hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript: no page builders, no bloated frameworks. Your CRM, calendar, and lead forms are wired and tested. Performance and accessibility are baked in from line one, not bolted on at the end.

  4. 04
    Days 18–21

    Launch & Handover

    Final QA across devices and browsers, a live walk-through with your team, and a clean handover package with documentation. Your site goes live on schedule. Post-launch support is included for 30 days.
