# Copy Voice and Vocabulary

## Preferred words
engineered, bespoke, architected, proprietary, precision-built, craftsmanship, qualified, concierge, reveal, deploy, specialist, curated, considered, authoritative

## Banned words
transformative, seamless, innovative, next-gen, easy, quick, cheap, free quote, unlock, supercharge, revolutionize, game-changer, cutting-edge, world-class, passionate, excited, leverage

## Headline formulas
Max 44 characters for hero H1
Formats: Benefit + Niche · Outcome + Method · Problem → Solution
Examples:
"Websites That Command Premium"
"Engineered for the Brands That Build Best"
"Your Craftsmanship. Finally Online."

## CTAs
Primary: "Request Your Concept Sprint"
Never: "Get Started" / "Contact Us" / "Learn More" / "Click Here"
Micro-copy below every CTA (12px, var(--text-2)):
"48-hour concept sprint" / "No long-term contract" / "Limited builds per quarter"

## Niche vocabulary

### Kitchen
cabinetry, countertops, hardware, pendant lighting, island, backsplash, soft-close, bespoke joinery, stone surfaces, integrated appliances

### Home Theater
acoustic panels, tiered seating, projection screen, surround speakers, Dolby Atmos, dedicated screening room, 4K projection, motorized blackout, isolation subfloor

### Custom Closet
shelving units, hanging rails, drawer modules, shoe displays, integrated lighting, wardrobe system, bespoke storage, cedar lining, soft-close drawers

### Bathroom
tiles, freestanding tub, vanity, shower enclosure, spa atmosphere, heated floors, rain head, steam room, limestone, bespoke wet room

### Windows and Doors
glazing, bi-fold doors, frames, conservatory, triple-glazed, sightlines, structural glass, aluminium frames, thermally broken, slim profile

## Pricing language
Use "Investment" not "Budget." Use "Concept Sprint" not "Discovery Call."
Use "Studio Services" not "Add-ons." Use "Virtual Concierge" not "chatbot."

## Rules
Outcomes over features always. Specific over vague always.
Never mention India, being solo, or being new.
Never sound like a freelancer. Always sound like a specialist studio.
Position as authority in the niche, not as a service provider.
